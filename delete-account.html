DocNum - Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your DocNum account (app by Abhishek Kumar Yadav) and your data.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open DocNum and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm (doctors: tap Request account deletion).

Option 2: Ask us by email (no app needed)

If you can no longer open the app, send us a request:

Email a deletion request

Please send the email from, or include, the mobile number or email address you used to register, so that we can find your account. We may reply to confirm it is you. We will process the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email, age details and login.
  • Your bookings and queue history.
  • For doctors: your profile, clinic details, photo and schedules, once the deletion is approved and you have no upcoming sessions or pending payments.

What may be kept

  • Payment and refund transaction records are kept for accounting and legal reasons. After deletion they are no longer linked to your profile.
  • Security and audit logs are kept for a limited period to prevent fraud.
  • If a doctor's past bookings are needed for other users' records, they may be kept without the doctor's personal details.

Deletion cannot be undone. See also our Privacy Policy.
