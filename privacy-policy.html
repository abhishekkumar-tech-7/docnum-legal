DocNum - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

DocNum ("the app", "we") is a mobile app that helps patients book queue tokens and appointment slots with doctors and clinics. The app is operated by Abhishek Kumar Yadav, India. You can contact us at krishnabhi04092026@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

WhoWhat we collectWhy
PatientsName, mobile number or email, password (stored securely by our login provider), the age of each patient you book for, booking history, payment statusTo create your account, book and manage tokens and slots, show your queue position, and process payments and refunds
DoctorsName, contact details, professional registration details, clinic name and address, profile photo, consultation fee, sessions and schedulesTo verify doctors, show them to patients, and manage their queues and earnings reports
ReceptionistsName, mobile number or email, the doctor who added themTo let a doctor's receptionist manage that doctor's queue only

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone data. We do not show advertisements and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Health-related information

When you book, the doctor and clinic you booked with can see your name, age, token or slot, and booking status so that they can run their queue. DocNum is a booking and queue tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and we do not store medical records, prescriptions or test reports.

3. Payments

Online payments are made by UPI through our payment partner, Cashfree Payments India Private Limited. You enter your UPI details only in your UPI app or on Cashfree's secure payment page. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card or bank details. We store only the order reference, amount and payment or refund status.

4. Who we share information with

  • The doctor or clinic you book with, as described above.
  • Service providers who help us run the app: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Cashfree (payments and refunds), and an SMS provider that delivers one-time login codes (OTP) to your mobile number.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Data is protected in transit using encryption (HTTPS).

5. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your account and booking history while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile and your bookings.
  • Payment and refund transaction records are kept for accounting and legal reasons. After account deletion they are no longer linked to your profile.
  • Security and audit logs that record important actions in the system are kept for a limited period to protect users and prevent fraud.
  • If a doctor's account is deleted, any past booking records that other users' histories depend on may be kept without the doctor's personal details.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile details in the app.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time. See our account deletion page.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask for a correction, by emailing us.

7. Children

DocNum is meant for adults. A parent or guardian may book on behalf of a child by entering the child's name and age. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
