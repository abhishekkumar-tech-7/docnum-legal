DocNum - Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your online payment when you cancel a booking, or when a booking cannot go ahead. It applies to doctor token and slot bookings and to lab test bookings made in the DocNum app, operated by UNSHA TECH.

1. Summary

SituationWhat happens to your online payment
You cancel in time (before your provider's cut-off)Refunded, less the provider's deduction if they have set one
You cancel after the cut-offNo refund
The doctor or lab closes or cancels the sessionRefunded in full
You do not arrive and are marked as skippedNo refund
Money was deducted but the payment did not completeBooking is confirmed, or the money is refunded automatically

2. When you cancel a booking yourself

  • Each doctor or lab sets a cancellation cut-off (for example, 2 hours before the session) and may set a deduction as a percentage of the refund. If a provider has not set their own rules, a platform cut-off applies and no deduction is made.
  • If you cancel at least that many hours before the session, you get a refund of what you paid online, less any deduction. If you cancel later, you are not refunded.
  • The app shows you the exact refund amount before you confirm a cancellation, and shows each provider's rule on their booking screen before you pay.
  • If you paid nothing online (you plan to pay at the clinic or lab), there is nothing to refund.

3. When the doctor or lab cancels

If a doctor or lab closes or cancels a session, or a holiday or block is added, your booking is cancelled and everything you paid online is refunded in full. You will get a notification in the app.

4. Skipped or missed appointments

If you are not present when your turn comes and the provider marks you as skipped, the online payment is not refunded.

5. Payment taken but booking not confirmed

Sometimes the bank debits money but the payment does not complete (for example, a network problem). In that case your booking is confirmed shortly after, or the money is refunded automatically. Please check My Bookings after a minute. If neither has happened within 24 hours, write to us at info@unshatech.com with the date, amount and your registered phone number.

6. How and when you get the money

  • Refunds go back to the same UPI account or payment method you paid with, through our payment partner Cashfree Payments. We cannot refund to a different account.
  • A refund starts as soon as the cancellation is processed. After that, banks usually take about 5 to 7 working days to show it in your account. Some may take longer.
  • You can see the refund amount and its status (pending, processing, completed or failed) under the booking in My Bookings.
  • If a refund fails, the app retries it automatically. If it still has not reached you, write to us and we will look into it.

7. Fees

The amount refunded is based on what you paid online for that booking, and is reduced only by the provider's deduction shown to you before you cancel. Any payment gateway or bank charge that a bank itself applies is outside our control.

8. Services already used

Once a consultation has been completed, or a lab test has been done and the report issued, the booking is considered used and is not refundable. If you have a complaint about the care or report, please raise it with the doctor or lab, and you may also write to us.

9. Contact us

For any refund or payment question, email info@unshatech.com with your registered phone number, the booking date and the amount.
